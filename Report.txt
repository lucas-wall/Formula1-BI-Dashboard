3.1. Team Report
The team report covers the full analysis of the consolidated Formula 1 data from 1950 to 2020. The team reviewed every aspect carefully, providing a holistic view of trends and performance over the years.

Each team member shared their personal experience and the lessons learned during the project. These reports highlight the different perspectives and individual contributions.

3.4. CONTEXT
The project started with the selection of a Kaggle dataset covering Formula 1 history from 1950 to 2020. The dataset is made up of 14 different tables, which were merged to build the file `formula1_merged_data.csv`.

3.5. METHODOLOGY
The methodology involved carefully merging the 14 tables, running exploratory analysis to identify and fix inconsistencies, and applying data visualization techniques in Power BI.

3.6. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION:
The results are presented through interactive Power BI visuals, discussing the main insights from the analysis, such as driver and team performance patterns across the decades.
